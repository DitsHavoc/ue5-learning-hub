# Designer Studio Game Diversity Pass — v3.35.2

## Why this pass exists

v3.35.0 introduced 24 real-game case-study placements, but those placements were concentrated across only seven games. The examples were individually strong, but the repetition made the Studio feel narrower than the design principles actually are.

v3.35.2 keeps the **three case studies per discipline** structure while making the LOOK layer deliberately broad:

- **24 case-study placements**
- **24 distinct games**
- **0 repeated games in the LOOK layer**
- industry deep dives may still revisit a game when the developer source itself is unusually useful

The goal is not novelty for novelty's sake. Each example has to demonstrate a different design job students can observe and transfer.

## Final 24-game spread

### Level Design Foundations
1. Portal 2 — visual language and route readability
2. Viewfinder — perspective and unusual spatial rules
3. Dishonored 2 — landmarks and multiple routes

### Environment Art & Set Dressing
4. Firewatch — visual economy and sense of place
5. Stray — lived-in spaces, signage and movement
6. The Long Dark — scarcity and meaningful props

### Materials & Surfaces
7. Death Stranding — surface identity and traversal
8. Teardown — material as gameplay rule
9. The Talos Principle 2 — material families and world logic

### Lighting & Atmosphere
10. Alan Wake 2 — colour and motivated emphasis
11. LIMBO — silhouette and negative space
12. Little Nightmares II — scale and pools of safety

### Landscape, Terrain & Foliage
13. ELDEN RING — distant landmarks and layered terrain
14. Subnautica — biome identity and vertical navigation
15. Jusant — terrain as traversal

### Sequencer & Cutscenes
16. Journey — camera, scale and emotion
17. What Remains of Edith Finch — transitions and point of view
18. Psychonauts 2 — stylised staging and readable action

### Audio & Sound Design
19. INSIDE — sparse sound and focus
20. Outer Wilds — music as state and place
21. No Man's Sky — procedural ambience and scale

### Polish, Optimisation & Presentation
22. Hi-Fi RUSH — cross-discipline coherence
23. Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart — transitions and readable spectacle
24. Ori and the Will of the Wisps — VFX and silhouette readability

## Teaching rule

The LOOK layer now follows this rule:

> **One shipped game = one primary design lesson.**

A game can appear again later in **HEAR THE DESIGNERS** when a documentary, interview or postmortem is genuinely the strongest professional source for that topic. That repetition is pedagogical rather than accidental.
