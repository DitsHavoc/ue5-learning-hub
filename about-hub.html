ABOUT THIS COURSE RESOURCE

Made for our classes.

The Learning Hub is a course tool built around what is actually taught in class. It changes when lessons, students and classroom use show that something can be clearer or better.

Why it exists

The Hub is there to support live teaching: catch up after an absence, repeat a demonstration, practise independently, find a quick Unreal or 3D reference, revise, or go further than the task set in class.

Who decides what goes in it?

Teaching decisionsCourse staff decide the learning sequence, tasks, examples, corrections and what students actually need.
Classroom testingIf a guide, image, button or explanation does not work in class, it gets changed.
Student feedbackRequests and problems raised by students have directly led to new tutorials, checks, guides and interface changes.
Real software referenceUnreal Engine and 3ds Max teaching uses real captures and authentic interface examples rather than invented UI pictures.

Was AI used?

Yes. AI-assisted coding and drafting have been used as development tools to help build and iterate the site faster. That is not the same as handing teaching over to AI. Course staff choose the structure, check the material, correct mistakes, test it in class and decide what stays.

The same principle applies to any production tool: usefulness comes from how it is directed, checked and improved. If something in the Hub is wrong or unhelpful, it should be challenged and fixed.

What matters to us

The finished resource should be useful, accurate and genuinely helpful in a lesson. Students are not expected to trust a page because it looks polished. Test the instructions, question explanations and tell us when something needs changing.
