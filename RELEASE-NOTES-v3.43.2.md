## New Theory lesson
**Greyboxing, Scale & Spatial Prototyping** in **Space & World**.

It teaches the WHY:
- greyboxing as a spatial prototype
- cheap changes before expensive art
- human scale and the player camera
- movement clearances
- affordances/readability
- playtest → observe → change → retest
- preserving the clean blockout for later comparison

The lesson includes a short prison-cell task, three knowledge-check questions,
a practical case study and current official Epic UE5.8 references.

## Prison Cell path
The opening now reads:

**PLAN → LEARN why greybox → MAKE with the teacher → PROVE in Play mode**

Existing Affordances & Signifiers and Playtesting & Iteration theory still appear
later, where those ideas become useful.

## Teaching model
- **Theory = why / design decisions**
- **Tutorial = how / tool operation**
- **Guided Path = connects both around something being made**

No app.js, backend.js, original Theory/Design/Pathway data, Supabase, XP,
roster controls, visual CSS or screenshot assets are replaced.
