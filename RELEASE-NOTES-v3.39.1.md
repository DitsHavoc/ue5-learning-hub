# UE5 Learning Hub v3.39.1 — Designer Evidence Pass

29 Aug 2026

## Why
The Designer Studio LOOK layer had the right games but too many cards still read like generic design prompts. A heading such as “Three examples. Three different design jobs.” did not tell a student what they were actually comparing, several tasks could be completed without understanding the game, and many primary source buttons simply opened Steam/store pages.

This pass makes the 24 primary case studies teach a **specific shipped design decision** and gives the student an evidence source worth reading or watching.

## What changed
- All **24 primary LOOK case studies** were rewritten.
- All **24 games remain unique** across the primary LOOK layer.
- Every case now names a concrete **DESIGN FACTOR** rather than a vague topic.
- Every case explains **WHAT [GAME] ACTUALLY DOES** in relation to that factor.
- Every case extracts a transferable **DESIGN PRINCIPLE**.
- Every case has a **STUDENT TASK • USE THE SOURCE** activity that requires the student to inspect the game/source and produce an annotation, map, comparison, storyboard or small design response.
- Every case now includes an **EVIDENCE** panel with source type, source title, why it is useful, and a direct Read/watch button.
- The generic “Three examples. Three different design jobs.” heading is removed. Each discipline now names the games and the exact comparison, e.g. “How Portal 2, Viewfinder and Dishonored 2 guide players through space.”

## Source policy
Primary LOOK links now prioritise the best evidence for the design decision:
1. developer/studio material, GDC talks and postmortems;
2. developer interviews and substantial editorial features from established games media;
3. store/product pages only as a last-resort context source.

The 24 current LOOK sources include Game Informer, Game Developer/GDC, Unreal Engine developer interviews, PlayStation Blog developer articles, Kotaku, Adobe/Remedy, Double Fine, Mobius Digital, Axios and Inverse. **0 of the 24 primary case-study evidence links point to Steam/store pages.**

Representative examples:
- Portal 2 — Valve's chamber workflow and why the art pass sends a puzzle back to playtesting when detail creates false clues.
- Stray — how Kowloon references and choosing a cat protagonist changed traversal and level design at cat scale.
- The Long Dark — how time, distance, weather and player decisions make quiet traversal tense.
- Jusant — how the climbing-first design avoids artificial painted ledge markers and relies on natural shape/colour cues.
- Ori and the Will of the Wisps — using greyscale/value separation to keep the player readable inside effects-rich scenes.

## Preserved
- Designer module IDs and routes.
- 24-game primary LOOK diversity.
- Designer tutorials, research missions, Studio Builds, Black Boxes and industry deep-dive completion IDs.
- XP values and badge logic.
- All Unreal, Modelling, Critique, Study Tools, Projects and account systems.
