# UE5 Learning Hub v3.57.7 — Visual Development Clarity Pass

## What changed
- Renamed the Level 4 2D route to **Visual Development — Design One Game Location**.
- Made it explicit that the location can be **INSIDE, OUTSIDE or IN-BETWEEN**.
- Added a prominent “What counts as one game location?” choice section.
- Rebalanced the six starter prompts: 2 interior, 2 exterior and 2 semi-open.
- Rewrote Stage 01 so students choose the type of place before they plan the brief.
- Added time/weather to the brief because it gives exterior and semi-open concepts useful visual direction.
- Removed room-biased wording from the thumbnail troubleshooting and final-image setup.
- Improved reference-board instructions for exterior as well as interior research.

The scope remains deliberately small: six rough thumbnails, two stronger directions, three value tests, three colour keys and ONE final concept image.
